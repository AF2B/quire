This site is the demo of quire. Everything you see was produced by the
generator from the files in `examples/demo/`.

| Piece | Where it lives |
|-------|----------------|
| Content | `content/*.md` |
| Site definition | `site.lisp` |
| Theme | `quire/theme-borba`, from the Borba Design System |

The output is deterministic: two builds of the same content are byte-identical.
